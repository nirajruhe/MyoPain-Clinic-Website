  • ▪ Persistent lower back discomfort
  • ▪ Neck tightness & postural ache
  • ▪ Sciatica & radiating nerve symptoms
  • ▪ Thoracic & upper back stiffness

  • ▪ Rotator cuff irritation & weakness
  • ▪ Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis)
  • ▪ Tennis & golfer's elbow
  • ▪ Repetitive strain & wrist tension

  • ▪ Hip impingement & groin strain

Assessment & Treatment Approach

Example evidence-based modalities that may be considered following an individualized clinical assessment:

01

Biomechanical Assessment

Comprehensive joint mobility, muscle length-tension, and postural movement screening to isolate root dysfunction.

02

Manual Therapy & Mobilization

Gentle joint mobilization, myofascial easing, and targeted soft tissue release to reduce stiffness and ease guarded motion.

03

Targeted Active Rehabilitation

Progressive corrective exercise therapy to restore muscle balance, motor control, and joint stability under load.

04

Self-Care & Ergonomic Guidance

Clear home exercise prescriptions, movement pacing, and workstation adjustments to sustain long-term relief.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions.

Helpful answers to common questions about preparing for your first physiotherapy session.

Your initial session begins with a comprehensive discussion about your symptoms, medical history, and daily activities. Your physiotherapist will conduct physical movement and joint tests to identify pain triggers. Together, you will outline a clear, realistic recovery plan.

In most cases, you can consult a physiotherapist directly for assessment and conservative care. If you have medical records, scan reports (X-ray/MRI), or a physician referral letter, please bring them along to assist our clinical evaluation.

Note: If claiming reimbursement from private health insurance, please check if your provider requires a physician's referral.

Wear comfortable, loose-fitting clothing that allows easy movement. For lower back, hip, or knee concerns, shorts or loose athletic pants are ideal. For neck and shoulder issues, a loose T-shirt or tank top allows clear visual assessment.

Initial evaluations typically take 45 to 60 minutes to ensure adequate time for assessment, explanation, and initial movement guidance. Follow-up sessions usually range between 35 to 45 minutes depending on your care plan.

Because recovery depends on tissue healing, symptom duration, overall health, and commitment to home exercises, session numbers vary for each individual. We establish milestone reviews and avoid scheduling unnecessary sessions once you can self-manage comfortably.

We accept UPI, major debit and credit cards, and cash payments. Itemized digital invoices are provided for tax or insurance claim purposes.
